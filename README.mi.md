# OpenAmer Agent

**Ko te kaihautū AI e whakapai ake ana i a ia anō — ako mai i te wheako, hanga ngā pūkaha, maumahara ki āu hiahia, me mahi mō koe i nga wāhi katoa.**

Whakamahia tētahi tauira i tōno aia — OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, me ētehi atu. Whakawhiti mā te `openamer model` — kāore he huringa wahi i te waehere.

## Ngā āhuatanga

- **Atamira tūmatara tūturu — TUI katoa me te whakapūmau i te kōrero, te hītōria, me te whakaputa taonga ara-pūreire**
- **E noho ana i ngā wāhi e noho ana koe — Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, me ētehi atu mai i tētahi kōpū kotahi**
- **Ako i te wā — te mahara, ngā pūkenga e whakapakari ana i a ia anō, te whakamahāra i ngā wāhanga rerekē**
- **Tūhono & whakatika — whakawhakaputa i ngā kaiāwhina iti mō te mahi tahi i te wā kotahi**
- **Ngā whakautoma whakatau — he cron i roto mō ngā pūrongo i aua rā, ngā hakaritanga, ngā tātari**
- **E rere ana i ngā wāhi katoa — i te mea ake, Docker, SSH, te kapua, serverless**

## Whakawhitinga tere

Windows (PowerShell):
```powershell
iex (irm https://github.com/openamer/openamer/raw/main/scripts/install.ps1)
```

Linux / macOS:
```bash
curl -fsSL https://github.com/openamer/openamer/raw/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
```

## Tīmatanga

```bash
openamer              # Tīmata te kōrero
openamer setup        # Tūhono i āu kī API me tō kaiwhakarite
openamer model        # Kōwhiria tō tauira
openamer update       # Whakahou ki te putanga hou rawa
```

## Whakahuahia

Ka kimi a OpenAmer i ngā whakaterea a-ake, ā, ka whakaatu he whakawewere i te pānui pōwhiri. Me whakahaere i te `openamer update` hei whiwhi i te putanga hou nhất — ka whakaawhi i ō tohutohu tuatahi.
