# OpenAmer Agent

**Ka mea kōkua AI e hoʻomanaʻo a e hoʻomaikaʻi i kona mākaukau—e aʻo mai ka ʻike, e hana i nā mākaukau, e hoʻomanaʻo i kou mau makemake, a e hana no ʻoe ma nā wahi a pau.**

E hoʻohana i kēlā me kēia modelo āu e makemake ai — OpenRouter, OpenAI, DeepSeek, a me nā mea ʻē aʻe. E hoʻololi me ka `openamer model` — ʻaʻole pono ke hoʻololi i ke kōd.

## Nā Mea Hana

- **Interface terminal maoli — TUI piha me ka hoʻopau ʻaukaʻi, ka moʻolelo, a me ka puka hōʻoluʻolu o nā mea hana**
- **Noho kahi e noho ai ana ʻoe — Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, a me nā mea ʻēleʻele mai i kahi puka kī kikoʻokiko hoʻokahi**
- **E aʻo ana i ka wā — ka hoʻomanaʻo, nā mīkini hoʻomaikaʻi ʻia, ka hoʻomanaʻo ʻana i nā wā aʻe**
- **Hoʻokuʻukuli & hoʻohui pū — hoʻokuʻu i nā kīkī-kīkī no ka hana pū ʻana**
- **Nā automation hoʻonohonoho ʻia — cron i haku ʻia no nā hōʻike lāʻau, nā backup, nā nānā ʻana (audits)**
- **Hana a i nā wahi a pau — local, Docker, SSH, cloud, serverless**

## Hoʻonohonoa Maikaʻi

Windows (PowerShell):
```powershell
iex (irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openamer/openamer/main/scripts/install.ps1)
```

Linux / macOS:
```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openamer/openamer/main/scripts/install.sh | bash
```

## Hoʻomaka ʻana

```bash
openamer              # E hoʻomaka i ka kamaʻilio ʻana
openamer setup        # Hoʻonohonoho i kāu mau kī API & ka mea hāpai
openamer model        # E koho i kou pola.
openamer update       # Hoʻonui i ka version hou loa
```

## Hoʻopouʻu

Huli automatically ka OpenAmer i nā hoʻonui hou a hōʻike ia i kahi ʻōlelo ʻeuʻu ma ka banner hoʻokipa. Hoʻohana i ka `openamer update` i mea e loaʻa ai ka version hou loa — e hana mua ia i ka backup o kāu mau ʻikepili.
